Privacy Policy

Last updated August 01, 2023

This privacy notice for Pixiux LLC (doing business as Pixiux) (“Pixiux,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we might collect, store, use, and/or share (“process”) your information when you use our services (“Services”), such as when you:

  • Visit our website at https://pixiux.com, or any website of ours that links to this privacy notice.
  • Engage with us in other related ways, including sales, marketing, or events.

Questions or concerns? Reading this privacy notice will help you understand your privacy rights and choices. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services. If you still have questions or concerns, please contact us at support@pixiux.com.

Summary of Key Points

  • What personal information do we process? We may collect personal information you provide to us, as well as certain technical data automatically when you visit or interact with our website and services.
  • Do we process sensitive personal information? No, Pixiux does not process sensitive personal information.
  • Do we receive information from third parties? No, we do not collect personal information from third parties.
  • How do we process your information? To provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, ensure security, and comply with laws.
  • Do we share information? We may share information with trusted service providers, partners, or during legal/business transitions.
  • How do we keep your data safe? We use organizational and technical safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security.
  • What are your rights? Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your data.

1. What information do we collect?

Personal information you disclose to us:

  • Name, email, phone number, job title, mailing address.
  • Provided when signing up, contacting us, or engaging in services.

Information automatically collected:

  • IP address, device type, browser, operating system, location, and usage activity.
  • Collected through cookies, log files, and analytics.

We do not process sensitive personal data.

2. How do we process your information?

  • Deliver and improve services.
  • Communicate with you (including customer support).
  • Ensure compliance and prevent fraud.
  • Run analytics, marketing, and promotional campaigns.
  • Meet legal or contractual obligations.

3. What legal bases do we rely on to process your personal information?

  • Your consent (e.g., subscribing to newsletters).
  • To fulfill a contract (e.g., providing requested services).
  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • For legitimate business interests (e.g., marketing, improving services).

4. When and with whom do we share your personal information?

  • Business transfers: mergers, acquisitions, or financing.
  • Service providers: hosting, email, CRM, analytics tools.
  • Business partners: joint services or promotions.

Pixiux does not sell personal information.

5. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

Yes. We use cookies, pixels, and tracking tools for website functionality, analytics/performance, and marketing personalization. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings.

6. How long do we keep your information?

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for providing services and meeting legal, tax, or accounting obligations. Once no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. How do we keep your information safe?

We apply organizational and technical safeguards. However, no online system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee complete protection against unauthorized access.

8. Do we collect information from minors?

Pixiux does not knowingly collect or process data from individuals under 18 years of age. If such data is collected unintentionally, it will be deleted.

9. What are your privacy rights?

  • Access your data.
  • Correct or update your data.
  • Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Opt out of marketing.

Requests can be submitted via support@pixiux.com.

10. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Currently, we do not respond to DNT browser signals due to lack of industry standards.

  • Right to opt out of data sale (Pixiux does not sell data).

Requests: https://pixiux.com/contact-us

12. Virginia Residents (CDPA)

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising or profiling.
  • Pixiux does not sell personal data.

13. Updates to this notice

We may update this Privacy Policy to remain compliant. Updates will be posted here with the revision date.

14. How can you contact us about this notice?

  • Email: support@pixiux.com
  • Contact: https://pixiux.com/contact-us

15. Review, update, or delete your data

You can review, update, or request deletion of your personal data by contacting us directly at support@pixiux.com.
